# Decodelabs-UI-UX-Project-4
Verdant Harvest- Nourishment Rooted in Real Living Soil

A clean, intuitive mobile shopping experience designed to make buying fresh, organic produce simple and convenient.

Verdant Harvest is a mobile grocery shopping app concept focused on helping users discover fresh produce, explore product details, manage their shopping basket, and complete their orders with ease. The design combines a natural visual identity with a structured shopping journey, creating an experience that communicates freshness, trust, and simplicity.

 📸 Project Preview

The project showcases the complete shopping journey, from the initial splash screen and product discovery to checkout and successful order placement.

Best experienced as a mobile shopping experience.

 🎯 Project Goal

The goal of this project was to design a seamless grocery shopping experience that allows users to browse products, make informed purchasing decisions, manage quantities, and complete checkout with minimal friction.

The interface focuses on:

•	Discovering and searching for fresh produce
•	Browsing products through categorized listings
•	 Viewing product details, descriptions, and prices
•	 Adjusting product quantities
•	 Managing the shopping basket and order summary
•	Selecting delivery addresses and delivery times
•	Choosing a payment method
•	 Completing checkout and viewing order confirmation

🔄 Shopping Journey

The design brings the core shopping experience together in a connected flow:

Splash → Search → Listings → Product Details → Checkout → Shopping Basket → Order Success

Each screen supports a stage of the customer journey, helping users move from product discovery to purchase completion.



🧩 Design Process

1. Research and Review

Reviewed the key requirements of a fresh-produce shopping experience and identified the screens needed to support product discovery, selection, and checkout.

2. User Flow Planning

Structured the shopping journey to guide users from browsing and searching for products through product selection, basket review, delivery, and payment.

3. UI Design

Developed a clean, high-fidelity interface using a natural colour palette, product imagery, clear typography, and organized content sections.

4. Interaction Design

Planned the key interactions around product selection, quantity controls, basket management, delivery options, payment selection, and order confirmation.

5. Prototyping

Connected the screens into a shopping journey designed to demonstrate how users move through the app and complete their purchases.

6. Refinement

Refined the visual hierarchy, spacing, product cards, buttons, and information layouts to maintain consistency and improve usability.

 ✨ Key Features

Product Discovery and Search

Users can search for groceries, explore product categories, and browse fresh produce through organized listings.

Product Details

Product pages present product imagery, descriptions, pricing, and quantity controls to help users make purchasing decisions.

Shopping Basket

The basket organizes selected products and displays quantities, item prices, delivery charges, and the order total.

Delivery and Payment

The checkout interface presents delivery address, delivery time, and payment method options before order placement.

Order Confirmation

The success screen confirms that the order has been placed and provides delivery information and follow-up actions.

🧭 Core User Flow

Discover → Search → Browse Products → View Product Details → Select Quantity → Review Basket → Choose Delivery → Select Payment → Place Order → Order Confirmation

🎨 Design Direction

The visual direction is clean, modern, minimal, and organic, reflecting the freshness and quality associated with natural produce.

The interface emphasizes:

	Freshness:Produce imagery and natural green accents
	Trust: Clear product information and transparent order summaries
	Simplicity:Straightforward navigation and organized layouts
	Quality: Consistent spacing, typography, and carefully structured product cards
	Convenience: A guided journey from product discovery to checkout

The overall experience aims to make grocery shopping feel clear, approachable, and effortless.

 🚀 Project Outcome

Verdant Harvest brings the essential stages of an online grocery purchase into one cohesive mobile interface, covering product discovery, product selection, basket management, checkout, and order confirmation.

The project demonstrates my approach to designing user-centred shopping experiences, with attention to visual consistency, information hierarchy, and the practical needs of users completing everyday transactions.

Key Takeaway

Good e-commerce design is not just about presenting products attractively. It is about helping users find what they need, understand their choices, and complete a purchase with confidence.
